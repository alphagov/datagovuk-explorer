# Collections vs Dataset pages: clicks comparison

Source: `data/pages 3 months.csv` (Google Search Console, 3 months)

|                | Collections | Datasets |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| Pages          | 88          | 27,258   |
| Total clicks   | 12,509      | 219,959  |
| Mean clicks    | 142.1       | 8.1      |
| Max clicks     | 3,280       | 7,771    |
| Zero-click pages | 0         | 6,232    |

Collection pages average ~17.6x more clicks than dataset pages (142 vs 8 per page). Dataset pages are far more numerous but most get very few or zero clicks.

## Excluding zero-click pages

|                | Collections | Datasets |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| Pages          | 88          | 21,026   |
| Total clicks   | 12,509      | 219,959  |
| Mean clicks    | 142.1       | 10.5     |
| Max clicks     | 3,280       | 7,771    |

Filtering to only pages with at least 1 click, collections still average ~13.5x more clicks per page (142 vs 10.5).
